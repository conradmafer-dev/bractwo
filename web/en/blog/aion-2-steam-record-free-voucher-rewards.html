AION 2: Steam record and a free Customization Voucher after launch

Bractwo Krain TeamOriginally published: 6 October 20263 min readEnglish version: 18 October 2026

Editorial update — 11 October 2026: after the launch records, NC announced restrictions on 4,996 accounts as part of its fight against bots and RMT. That is a later development; the article below covers the Steam result and gifts announced on 5 October. Check their current availability in the game.

Following AION 2's global launch, the game reached a record of 397,905 concurrent players on Steam on 5 October. Korean outlet Inven covered the result the following morning. For players arriving in Atreia, the official gift announcements are equally useful: a cosmetic voucher and items celebrating successive player-count milestones.

Information as of: 6 October 2026, 06:59 Poland time (CEST).
Steam record: 397,905 concurrent players, recorded on 5 October.
Cosmetic gift: Customization Voucher (Bound), announced on 5 October at 21:02 Poland time.

How many people are playing AION 2 after launch?

SteamDB shows a historical peak of 397,905 concurrent players on 5 October. This measures players using Steam. Those launching the game through the PURPLE client are not included in that statistic.

Inven's morning report was published on 6 October at 11:04 in Korea, or 04:04 in Poland (CEST). The outlet compares the result with 171,530 players during Early Access: an increase of 226,375, or roughly 2.3 times the earlier figure. A separate official team post thanks players for passing 400,000 active users.

NC uses the term “active users”, while the SteamDB chart shows concurrent players on one platform. The figures have different scopes and should not be added together.

The record captures the largest number recorded at a single moment. The live count changes with the time of day; you can check it on the linked chart. The result alone does not reveal queue times on a particular server.

Free Customization Voucher: where can you use it?

In the voucher announcement for all players, NC said it would send a Customization Voucher (Bound). The post was published on 5 October at 19:02 UTC, or 21:02 CEST. The gift responds to players' interest in appearance and character skin changes.

The developers give the menu path ESC → Closet. We retain the English menu name so you can find it in the game client. Before confirming a change, read the received item's description and the message in the cosmetic customization window.

Check the current item description and in-game notices for claiming conditions and any voucher expiry date. The announcement confirms the gift and the Closet menu, but does not give a claim deadline or a complete list of usage restrictions.

Which gifts were announced for 300,000 and 400,000 players?

The official thank-you for 400,000 active users lists two gifts:

  • Resurrection Spiritstone (Bound) ×15 — the reward accompanying the 400,000 milestone.
  • Artisan’s Roasted Fogus (Bound) ×15 — the earlier 300,000 gift, also described in this post.

The announcement was published on 5 October at 16:04 Poland time (CEST). These are gifts announced by the game team. The linked notices do not include a redemption code or a requirement to watch Twitch streams. Check current AION 2 notifications for availability on your account.

Is AION 2 already free to play?

The global free-to-play PC launch took place on 5 October at 15:00 Poland time (CEST). The game's Steam page labels AION 2 as Free To Play and lists in-app purchases. Free access to the game does not mean that every item or service is free.

If you previously looked up the server opening time, the schedule and launch changes are covered in our earlier article about the AION 2 launch in Poland time. This article adds the launch-day result and the specific gifts.

A later announcement, dated 8 October, covered the new EU servers Nathara and Tassin and planned maintenance. That article also details the announced compensation for the European server outage.
