VAMPIR classes: which to choose, combat and progression

Bractwo Krain TeamOriginally published: 22 October 2026English version: 22 October 2026Updated: 14 November 2026

VAMPIR offers five classes. Start by comparing weapons, range and combat style, then look at the abilities you want to develop. Below is a selection table and a practical explanation of the systems documented by Netmarble. The global release launched on 28 October 2026 at 02:00 UTC.

VAMPIR classes at a glance

Weapons and combat styles come from the global class presentation and Netmarble’s English-language class guide. The last column is our selection advice based on those descriptions.

These differences help narrow the choice. They do not tell you which class will deal the most damage with your equipment. The official descriptions alone cannot establish a best class for PvP, farming or playing without spending.

Which class should you choose first?

If you want a gunslinger, start with Carnage. If you want magic, look at Viper. For melee combat, compare Akasha’s tonfas and pursuit theme, Bloodstained’s sword and Grim Reaper’s scythe. This is a choice of preferred style rather than a rating of current seasonal strength.

Before committing valuable materials, read the individual ability description, the class requirement on its Skill Book and the effects of the Bloodshape you want to use. Check when the ability can be used and whether it supports the way you want to fight.

The class guide specifically describes Akasha stealing an opponent’s ATK and removing debuffs even while stunned. This gives you a mechanic to inspect in the skill descriptions; the guide does not provide a measured comparison against other classes.

Skills: books, active abilities and automatic combat

The official skill guide distinguishes active and passive abilities. Active skills go into quick slots and can be used manually or automatically. Settings let you adjust their order and usage cycle. Passive skills take effect after being learned.

You learn a skill by using a class-appropriate book from your inventory. The guide lists purchases with Gold in villages, crafting, bosses, player trading and quests as sources. This does not mean every skill is obtainable through every one of those methods.

  1. Check the book. Confirm that it matches your class and read what the skill does.
  2. Set your quick slots. Assign a new active ability to a suitable slot; learning it does not replace configuring its use.
  3. Review automatic use. Set order and frequency for the opponents you are fighting, then assess changes during play.

Bloodshape: appearance, stats and class compatibility

Bloodshapes can change your character’s appearance and grant stats and skills. Netmarble distinguishes class-exclusive shapes from common ones. Its guide also explains duplicate fusion and collection effects.

Compare the effects and class requirements before choosing. The artwork does not tell you which abilities you will gain. The guide separates an equipped shape from the appearance you select: applying the look of an owned shape does not automatically add its ordinary stats; special appearances can have separate effects.

Clans: what can you do with other players?

The clan content guide describes three kinds of group activity:

  • Clan Missions: timed boss encounters with rewards for participants and the clan.
  • Relic Defense: protecting a relic against monster waves and competing for a score.
  • War of Occupation: clan matches in which occupying altars and defeating monsters earn points.

When choosing a clan, ask about its language, play times and activity expectations. Check event availability and entry requirements in the current client and announcements for your region.

Does choosing a class solve the spending question?

VAMPIR is free to play with purchases. The publisher’s Epic Games Store description mentions hunting for Diamonds and Trinity’s role in crafting and trading. It also explicitly connects progression to time spent playing and money spent; the listing flags paid random items.

Choosing a class alone cannot support a promise that you will compete with every opponent without spending. If you intend to play for free, check what an item provides, which goal it serves and which in-game acquisition methods are available before buying it or consuming resources.

Nocturne: where does the story take place?

In the official world story, Nocturne is an empire of night built by Cain and his bloodline. Conflict with Lillai and the Ecclesiac brings down his power. Survivors take refuge in Krvna, where Olga receives a prophecy about a successor carrying Cain’s blood.

This premise explains the vampire theme of the classes and setting. Your choice of game can also reflect whether gothic environments and a story about warring bloodlines appeal to you.

First steps for a new player

  1. Choose a class by weapon and preferred combat style using the table above.
  2. Read your basic abilities and review their automatic-use settings.
  3. Check Skill Book and Bloodshape class compatibility before spending materials.
  4. Look for a clan whose play times and pace fit yours.
