Best mobile MMORPGs: which one should you play on Android?

Bractwo Krain TeamOriginally published 28 November 2026English edition 28 November 2026

Pick Old School RuneScape if you want quests and skills on a phone with limited storage. Choose Albion Online if gathering, trading and real-time fights sound like a good evening. Broken Ranks suits a player who wants a story and time-limited tactical turns. For a character whose combat abilities you can arrange on an action bar, choose modern RuneScape. All four let you carry on with the same character on PC.

Can you control combat yourself?

Broken Ranks gives you a short planning window each round. Divide points between attacks and defence, then watch the choices made by both sides resolve. Even if you love turn-based RPGs, check whether you enjoy committing to a plan before the timer runs out.

Albion Online gives you direct control of movement and abilities during real-time combat. Touch controls include targeting assistance. Try switching between ordinary monsters first, before taking expensive gear into a zone where you can lose it.

In OSRS, turn off Auto Retaliate in Combat Options if you want to decide whether to fight back when attacked. Once you select an enemy, regular attacks repeat. In RS3, Revolution can use abilities from a portion of your action bar in order. Try Full Manual if you want to choose when to use each ability.

How do you pick up the same character on PC?

Make a note of the sign-in method and server you chose on your phone. In Albion Online and Broken Ranks, sign in on PC and select that server to find your character. For RuneScape, match the edition too. If you have been playing OSRS on Android, open OSRS on your computer. Launching RS3 opens a separate profile with its own equipment and skills.
