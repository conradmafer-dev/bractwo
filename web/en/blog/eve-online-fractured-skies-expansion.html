EVE Online: Fractured Skies — release date, navy ships and the road to Conquests

Bractwo Krain TeamOriginally published: 20 October 2026English version: 20 October 20265 min read

EVE Online: Fractured Skies launches on 10 November 2026. Its announcement sets out a campaign extending into 2027. For returning pilots, the useful question is which features arrive first and which require a longer commitment. Here is the schedule, the role of the new ships and a practical way to approach the expansion.

The schedule announced on 20 October:

  • 10 November 2026: expansion launch; ships, rigs, campaigns and the first HUD preview.
  • 5 January 2027: campaign bonuses activate in designated faction systems.
  • Later in January: empire showdown.
  • February 2027: first Conquest.

These are the developer’s announced milestones. Conquests are a later stage of the expansion cycle.

Which navy attack battlecruisers are coming?

The official Fractured Skies announcement names four hulls, available through Factional Warfare LP stores:

  • Naga Navy Issue: cruise missiles and torpedoes replace hybrid weapons.
  • Tornado Fleet Issue: stronger projectile firepower and harder to hit under microwarpdrive.
  • Oracle Navy Issue: improved optimal range.
  • Talos Navy Issue: improved mobility and falloff.

Our reading: start with the job you want a ship to do. A missile Naga deserves a fresh fitting discussion rather than an automatic copy of a hybrid Naga setup. Likewise, range and mobility bonuses should be evaluated against your group’s preferred engagement distance. The announcement is a starting point for theorycrafting; final fittings need the released hull statistics, available skills and a realistic replacement budget.

What do Synchronizer rigs change?

The publisher’s launch release describes nine Synchronizer rig sets, covering four empires and five pirate factions. Matching faction modules strengthen their effects; pirate versions bring greater drawbacks.

For fitting decisions, our advice is to compare the complete setup rather than the largest displayed bonus. Ask what you give up to satisfy the faction synergy: another module, flexibility, or a cheaper replacement. Keep an ordinary alternative beside the experimental fit. That comparison will remain useful after the first week’s enthusiasm, particularly when your fleet needs several pilots to afford the same doctrine.

Why does the roadmap continue into 2027?

Conquests are planned as seasonal Factional Warfare conflicts ending in a siege: defend your empire’s Conquest Engine and destroy the opponent’s. November’s preparation and February’s first Conquest therefore serve different purposes.

For a returning corporation, we would treat the gap as planning time. Reconnect with fleet organizers, decide which activities members actually enjoy and set a sustainable budget before committing to a new doctrine. Someone who can join one evening a week needs a clearer plan than “be ready for the expansion.” Agree on the first activity and a meeting point; revisit the plan when the detailed rules arrive.

The same press release also confirms a separate paid EVE Vanguard alpha from 10 November. Its access is separate from EVE Online’s Omega subscription. Check the eventual access terms before budgeting for both games.

Is there a useful starting point for new pilots?

Fractured Skies adds 13 free, pre-fitted AIR Academy ships for combat, exploration and mining. They remain inside Starter Space. The earlier Cradle of War FAQ explains the wider context: Exordium blocks PvP, offers introductory activities and allows veterans to visit and help. Rewards are lower than outside this training area.

Our suggestion for a newcomer is to use that space to discover a preferred activity before buying into a fleet plan. Try to describe what you liked: managing range, finding sites, gathering resources, or working with other pilots. Bring that answer to a group you might join. For a mentor, one focused lesson followed by a small independent task is easier to assess than a tour of every system at once.

What is changing in the EVE Online HUD?

The first redesign is an optional Feature Preview. The HUD development blog explains the initial focus: ship status, capacitor information, module cycles and overload controls. It separates those foundations from later work on the minimap, consumables and customization.

We would test the preview during a familiar, low-pressure activity. Check whether you can read your defenses, recognize an active module and find the overload control without searching. Take notes about one interaction at a time. A concept image showing a future feature is not confirmation that the first November version includes it.
