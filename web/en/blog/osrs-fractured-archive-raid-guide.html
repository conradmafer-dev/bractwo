OSRS The Fractured Archive guide: requirements, teams and rewards

Bractwo Krain TeamOriginally published: 20 October 2026English version: 20 October 20265 min read

The Fractured Archive, Old School RuneScape’s fourth raid, launched on 20 October 2026. Its lasting appeal is a group challenge with a choice of encounters and new equipment to pursue. Before organising a run, understand the quest requirement, how the party scales and what a failed attempt costs.

  • Required quest: While Guthix Sleeps.
  • Team size: scaling from two to eight players; Jagex recommends three to five.
  • Hardcore accounts: deaths are unsafe.
  • Launch status: Practice Mode was unavailable on 20 October; its later activation was planned separately.

Requirements and how to reach the raid

The story follows the events of While Guthix Sleeps. After completing it, look for the archive entrance on the cliff near the Guthixian Temple. The launch announcement describes a messenger who directs eligible players there; the entrance can also be reached directly.

The quest unlock matters more than following release-day excitement. If your account cannot complete its prerequisite chain, assemble a quest plan before buying a raid loadout. Jagex discussed accommodating restricted builds but had not delivered those changes for launch: a future possibility does not remove the current requirement.

Choosing a team and using the lobby

The Grouping Board on the eastern side lets you create a party and accept applicants. The Strategy Board to the west supports spectating, provided a party permits spectators. That makes watching a group useful preparation for understanding roles before you spend supplies.

Jagex’s recommended size is three to five, within a two-to-eight scaling range. Solo attempts are possible, but are scaled for two players; the developers describe them as an advanced challenge. A duo should likewise expect fewer people to share responsibilities. For a learning group, our advice is to agree on voice communication, time available and a replacement plan before entering. A larger roster only helps if everyone knows what they are expected to do.

Boss route: three choices between a fixed start and finish

You encounter the Archive Guardian first, then choose one encounter from each of three pairs before reaching the final boss. The choices are fixed rather than randomly scouted:

  1. Bork or Ezekiel.
  2. Dreadnoth the Jellied or the necromancer.
  3. Xarth or the Dagon’hai Trio.

Jagex says each pairing awards equal total contribution points. Choosing one door does not inherently improve the unique reward chance for that completion. Our practical reading: use consistency as your first benchmark. A route your team finishes reliably can be more productive than repeatedly wiping on a theoretically faster alternative. Individual contribution and completion speed still matter; equal points do not promise identical results per hour.

Deaths, wipes and the launch limitation on practice

A team wipe ends the attempt and puts a gravestone outside. Gravestone fees are reduced by 75% until you reach five completions. This discount does not make a death safe: HCIM and HCGIM accounts lose their Hardcore status if they die inside.

On 20 October 2026, Practice Mode was not available. The announced later system would let players rehearse bosses or phases they had already encountered, with supplies refunded. Treat that as a separate feature to check in subsequent patch notes, rather than assuming the launch build offers a risk-free rehearsal.

For early sessions, set aside a learning budget and decide when to stop. Recording which mechanic caused each wipe gives the next attempt a concrete purpose. This preparation advice is our assessment, not a claim that we have cleared the raid.

Which rewards are worth understanding?

The reward pool serves different goals. Think about how an item fits your account rather than treating every new drop as a universal upgrade:

  • Zeal: an upgrade to Piety; the unlock adds five percentage points to its Attack and Strength bonuses.
  • Rondache: a shield that reduces incoming Melee, Ranged and Magic damage by two, storing the prevented damage for a Shield Bash.
  • Zorya’s Tome: a two-handed magic weapon with a special attack built around a burst of faster, empowered hits.
  • Ascension Crossbows: dual-wield weapons using Ascension bolts, pitched by Jagex as a raid-tier progression from the Toxic Blowpipe.
  • Obligator: the rare Crush weapon, whose damage rerolls reward exploiting the target’s relative Crush weakness.

There are also untradeable Elemental Fragments and the Archivist’s Folio teleport item. Ascension shards support equipment and teleport upkeep. Compare a prospective upgrade with the content you actually play; new raid equipment does not automatically replace every existing setup. We do not assign drop rates, market values or profit per hour that the cited launch information cannot establish.

A sensible first-session plan

Complete the quest, gather a stable team, inspect the lobby and watch an encounter before committing to a long session. Then aim for a repeatable route and shared understanding of failures. Exact gear rankings can evolve as players learn; the access rules and group systems are the better starting point for deciding whether this raid suits you.
