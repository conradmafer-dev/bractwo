AION 2: new EU servers Nathara and Tassin, plus 9 October maintenance

Bractwo Krain TeamOriginally published: 8 October 20264 min readEnglish version: 18 October 2026

Editorial update — 11 October 2026: a later report covers action against bots and RMT, with 4,996 AION 2 accounts restricted. Below, we retain the 8 October announcements about servers, compensation and the maintenance schedule for 9 October; they do not describe the current service status.

NC announced two new European servers for AION 2: Nathara for Elyos and Tassin for Asmodians. The 8 October notice scheduled their opening for 18:00 Poland time (CEST). NC also announced maintenance on 9 October and higher Flight Power for wings earned through quests.

Key dates and times in Poland (CEST):
New EU servers: 8 October, 18:00 — the time announced by NC.
All-service maintenance: 9 October, 08:30, scheduled to last 90 minutes.
Update: the compensation announcement was published at 19:17. Check the rewards and affected servers.
Sources checked: 8 October 2026, 19:49 CEST.

New AION 2 servers: which factions use Nathara and Tassin?

NC's announcement of new European servers was published on 8 October at 17:00 Poland time (CEST). Its schedule assigns a separate server to each faction:

EU serverFactionAnnounced opening
NatharaElyos8 October, 18:00 CEST
TassinAsmodian8 October, 18:00 CEST

The notice gives 09:00 PDT / 18:00 CEST. Poland also observed CEST on that date. When the sources were checked, no separate confirmation that the opening had taken place had been published; the table above presents NC's schedule.

Can you transfer a character to a new server?

In its 8 October notice, NC encouraged players to join the new servers and announced character transfers in the future, allowing friends to reunite. That announcement did not yet provide a release date or the transfer conditions.

11 October update: NC has since published the rules and dates: 14–21 October for Early Access servers and 21–28 October for launch servers. Check the subsequently announced free AION 2 transfer schedule and its restrictions before planning a character move.

The developers also note that instances, including dungeons, support cross-server play. When choosing where to start, distinguish between entering an instance together and moving an existing character. If you want to begin on the same server as your friends, agree on a faction and server name beforehand.

AION 2 maintenance on 9 October: Poland times

The temporary maintenance notice, published on 8 October at 15:01 Poland time, schedules the start for 9 October at 08:30 CEST. The downtime covers all services and is expected to last 1 hour 30 minutes.

That puts the expected end at around 10:00 CEST; this time is calculated from the stated duration. The team asks players to log out in a safe location before maintenance. The work covers server stabilization and the wing changes detailed below.

Quest wings to gain 500 Flight Power each

The update is intended to increase the Flight Power of each of the four quest reward wings by 500. The change applies to their Elyos and Asmodian equivalents:

WingsBeforeAfter
Daeva’s Lesser Wings20002500
Daeva’s Intermediate Wings5001000
Daeva’s Superior Wings5001000
Daeva’s Ultimate Wings5001000

NC's reference to “2,000 in total” means the sum of four increases of 500. The notice does not announce a single +2,000 bonus for every character.

AION 2 EU outage compensation: what do players receive?

The issue was resolved, and on 8 October at 19:17 Poland time (CEST), NC published EU outage compensation details. Players on affected servers with an active membership are to receive 1 additional membership day; those with active pet auto loot are to receive 1 extra day of that service. The extensions are scheduled for the next maintenance, with no action required from players.

A Special Daeva Supply chest is also to be delivered through in-game mail, containing the following items. All listed items are Bound:

Elyos servers covered by compensation: Nezekan, Kaisinel, Ariel, Meslamtaeda, Nania, Luteros, Daminu, Bakarma, Kochi, Tiamat.
Asmodian servers: Zikel, Lumiel, Azphel, Beritra, Ulgorn, Odar, Kromede, Baba, Fafnir, Agnita, Atiel.
