VAMPIR download: PC, Android and iOS requirements

Bractwo Krain TeamOriginally published: 18 October 2026English version: 18 October 2026Updated: 14 November 2026

VAMPIR is available on PC, Android and iOS. The global release launched on 28 October 2026 at 02:00 UTC — 03:00 in Poland — as confirmed by Netmarble’s post-launch announcement. Here are the official downloads, minimum requirements and language details.

The global VAMPIR website links to these distribution channels. On the verification date, Epic lists the game as free with a Get button, Google Play offers installation and the App Store has a released app listing. You can use these channels to begin playing without waiting for pre-registration.

Netmarble confirms cross-platform play between PC and mobile devices. When changing devices, check the correct account and server in the client.

The Epic listing we checked does not specify required disk space. Check your free storage before installing, allowing for additional data downloaded by the client.

  • Android: the Google Play description checked for Poland lists Android 11 as the minimum. The store on your device determines compatibility with your particular model.
  • iPhone and iPad: the App Store requires iOS 15.0 or iPadOS 15.0 or later. The listed app size may not include all data downloaded during play.

Does VAMPIR support English or Polish?

English appears in the language lists checked on Epic Games Store and the Polish App Store. Polish does not. A translated storefront page does not itself mean the game has that language.

When did VAMPIR launch globally?

Netmarble confirmed the global release at 19:00 PDT on 27 October 2026. That was 02:00 UTC on 28 October and 03:00 in Poland, after Poland’s October clock change.

This expanded the game into more markets after earlier Asian releases. Store package dates can differ from the time servers open. The Polish App Store now lists a released app, so the earlier discrepancy in the expected iOS date no longer means waiting for launch.

Is VAMPIR free, and how should you start?

This Netmarble Neo MMORPG is set in a gothic world of vampires. It is free to start and includes purchases. The publisher’s Epic description links progression to time spent playing and money spent; the listing also flags paid random items. The free-to-play label alone does not establish the size of any paying-player advantage.

After installing, choose a class by weapon and combat style. Our VAMPIR class guide compares Akasha, Carnage, Bloodstained, Viper and Grim Reaper and explains the basics of skills and Bloodshape.

The launch announcement described rewards and introductory events. Check their current dates and claim conditions in current VAMPIR announcements and your in-game mail; an archived announcement does not confirm that every reward remains available today.
