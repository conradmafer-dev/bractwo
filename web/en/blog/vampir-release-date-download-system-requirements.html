VAMPIR release date: Poland launch time, download and requirements

Bractwo Krain TeamOriginally published: 18 October 2026English version: 18 October 20263 min read

VAMPIR, Netmarble’s vampire MMORPG, is scheduled to launch globally on 28 October at 03:00 in Poland (CET, UTC+1). The publisher has announced PC and mobile versions with cross-platform play. Here are the dates, official links and requirements to check before launch.

The announcement was published on 16 October 2026. The times below use the time zone in effect in Poland on launch day: CET (UTC+1).

  • Pre-download: 27 October at 03:00 CET.
  • Announced global launch: 28 October at 03:00 CET.

Why does VAMPIR launch on 28 October in Poland?

Netmarble’s announcement gives 27 October at 19:00 PDT. In Poland, that will be Wednesday, 28 October at 03:00 CET. Pre-download is scheduled to begin exactly one day earlier. This conversion accounts for Poland’s October clock change.

A note for iPhone users: when we checked the sources on 18 October, the Polish App Store listing showed an expected date of 29 October. This differs from the publisher’s schedule. The date refers specifically to the Polish App Store listing; check the current availability of the iOS version before launch.

Where can you download VAMPIR for PC, Android and iOS?

The official website links to the following stores. Before downloads open, these pages are for pre-registration or wishlisting:

  • VAMPIR for PC — Epic Games Store.
  • VAMPIR for Android — Google Play.
  • VAMPIR for iPhone and iPad — App Store (Poland).

Official VAMPIR website and pre-registration ↗

Pre-registration rewards include a Heroic Mount and 100 million gold. The official website states that rewards are limited to once per account and must be claimed within 30 days of being granted. The pre-registration event itself is for adults.

VAMPIR minimum PC and mobile requirements

According to the current Epic Games Store listing, a PC should meet these minimum requirements:

  • Operating system: Windows 10.
  • Processor: Intel Core i5, 3.0 GHz — no generation specified.
  • Memory: 16 GB RAM.
  • Graphics: NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1060.
  • DirectX: version 11.

The listing does not specify the storage space required. The App Store lists iOS 15 or iPadOS 15 as the minimum operating system. To check compatibility with a particular Android phone, open Google Play on that device.

Polish language support: Polish is absent from the current list of supported languages on the Epic Games Store. English is among the announced languages.

What kind of MMORPG is VAMPIR, and is it free?

VAMPIR is developed by Netmarble Neo and set in a gothic world of vampires. The October launch expands availability to more markets following earlier Asian releases. It gives players in Poland another MMORPG to try on PC or mobile.

The game uses a free-to-play model with purchases. The publisher’s description explicitly links spending to character progression, and the Epic listing also indicates paid random items. Keep this in mind when choosing a game to play long term.
