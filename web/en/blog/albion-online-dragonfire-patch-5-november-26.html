Albion Online moves Dragonfire Patch 5 to November 26

Bractwo Krain TeamOriginally published: 26 November 2026English version: 26 November 20263 min read

Dragonfire Patch 5 is coming to Albion Online earlier than planned. A new notice schedules maintenance across all servers for November 26 at 10:00 UTC, or 11:00 CET. If you are planning a Thursday session, here is the schedule and a short look at the test-server changes.

Source date: MostlyBread’s official forum notice was published on 25 November 2026 at 18:02 CET (17:02 UTC). Our article is published on November 26.

November 26 maintenance: time and scope

The team attributes the earlier release to a technical issue requiring an immediate fix. That fix will be combined with the planned update. The notice does not describe the underlying fault.

Scheduled maintenance, November 26, 2026
ItemDetails
Start10:00 UTC / 11:00 CET
ServersAll servers
DurationApproximately one hour — the team’s estimate

Maintenance is still upcoming when this article is published. An approximate 11:00 UTC / 12:00 CET reopening follows from that estimate; it is not a confirmed reopening time. Check the current server notice before starting a longer activity.

What do the Patch 5 notes cover?

The schedule announcement links to the test-server patch notes and test-server combat changes. Both threads were originally published on November 9. They provide context for the new release date and still describe a test version ahead of maintenance.

Four changes described in the test notes
AreaDescribed change
Dragon SanctuaryDouble the overall loot value in Azaryon’s chest, with greater increases at higher tiers.
Spell barChange a spell by right-clicking its slot.
ArmorySeparate Ancient Lands and Dragon Sanctuary categories.
Combat balance15% resilience penetration for all cloth armor.

The notes state that Dragon Sanctuary PvE leaderboard tracking begins with this patch, without recovering earlier results. Allow for that fresh starting point when comparing your group’s activity later.

What should you change in your plans?

Our practical suggestion: update your guild calendar and include CET or UTC. With an international roster, “at eleven” can mean different things. Leave some margin before an activity requiring the whole group instead of making its start depend on the estimated end of maintenance.

Once servers reopen, check the final notes and new interface options. For Dragon Sanctuary organisers, we suggest comparing several runs alongside equipment costs and participant numbers. A higher chest value does not determine one player’s earnings. PvP players can review the changes affecting their own equipment before their next ranked fight.

The earlier release leaves less time for preparation. Start with the changes affecting the activity you actually plan after maintenance; the full lists remain available in the official threads.
