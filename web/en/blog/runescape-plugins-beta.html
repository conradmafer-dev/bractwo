RuneScape plugin beta on 6 October: Quest Helper supports 62 quests

Bractwo Krain TeamOriginally published: 4 October 20263 min readUpdated: 12 October 2026English version: 18 October 2026

Official RuneScape plugins were scheduled to enter testing on 6 October 2026. Jagex confirmed the date during RuneFest and named five plugins for the initial test. These include Quest Helper, whose first version would support 62 quests.

Announcement: 3 October 2026. Scheduled beta start: 6 October 2026. As of 4 October, testing had not begun; instructions for accessing beta worlds were due on launch day. This article preserves the original announcement rather than reporting the current beta status.

Quest Helper and four other plugins

The announcement concerns RuneScape, the game often referred to as RS3. Jagex points to the popularity of plugins in Old School RuneScape, but the announced test concerns a different version of the game. It is also separate from testing for the new RS4 project.

The first beta was set to include:

  • Quest Helper — a questing assistant with initial support for 62 quests.
  • Menu Entry Swapper.
  • Tilemarkers.
  • Entity Hider.
  • Job Gauges.

The plugins were developed by community members working with Jagex. The limited selection was intended to make it easier to test the system before expanding it. The number 62 describes Quest Helper's initial coverage; it does not mean that every quest in the game is supported.

Where will the RS Plugin Hub be?

The announced RS Plugin Hub will be accessible in-game through a toolbox icon on the Ribbon menu in the bottom-right corner of the screen. In the legacy interface, the icon is intended to be visible by default; in the modern interface, access depends on how the Ribbon is configured.

After installing a plugin, players will also need to enable it in the Manage tab. The Hub is intended to support plugin management and issue reporting. This describes the planned plugin controls rather than providing complete beta access instructions: Jagex said details about test worlds would arrive on Tuesday, 6 October.

Update, 12 October 2026: this article covers the testing announcement published on 3 October. For the game's broader direction, see our RuneScape 2027 roadmap overview: Dragon Slayer II, Agility and Arposandra.

Why does this matter for returning RuneScape players?

Our take: quest assistance could reduce the need to keep switching between the game and a guide. For returning players, the number of plugins matters less than whether they support the quests they are currently playing and whether hints are easy to turn off.

The beta would show how these tools work in practice. Before trying them, it is worth choosing a specific quest and checking whether the helper makes navigation easier without taking away the pleasure of discovery.
