AION 2 global launch on 5 October: what time did servers open in Poland?

Bractwo Krain TeamOriginally published: 5 October 20263 min readEnglish version: 18 October 2026

Editorial update — 11 October 2026: looking for later post-launch news? We covered NC's action against bots and RMT, which placed restrictions on 4,996 AION 2 accounts. Below, we retain the historical launch schedule from 5 October; the newer article covers a separate event.

AION 2 was preparing for its global free-to-play PC launch on 5 October. According to the schedule, maintenance was expected to end around 15:00 Poland time (CEST). That morning, the developers published a thank-you to Early Access participants and welcomed players joining at launch.

Information as of: 5 October 2026, 09:15 CEST.
Scheduled end of maintenance: 15:00 in Poland (CEST).
Status at original publication: scheduled maintenance was underway, and its end time could change.

What time were AION 2 servers scheduled to open in Poland?

The official notice sets the start of maintenance at 07:00 CEST and allows eight hours for the work. This gives a scheduled server return time of 15:00 CEST, or 13:00 UTC. Login is unavailable during maintenance. The update is intended to end Advanced Access and begin the commercial launch.

The 15:00 time is calculated from the stated maintenance duration. Before launching the game, check the current AION 2 news on Steam: confirmation that maintenance has ended is a better signal than the clock alone. Even after 15:00, follow the developers' current announcement.

The global launch took place on 5 October at 15:00 Poland time (CEST). We cover what followed in our article on the Steam player record and post-launch gifts, originally published on 6 October.

What does the launch update change?

  • Character deletion: the waiting period drops from 168 to 24 hours, and the required level drops from 10 to 1.
  • Quest objects: some will stop disappearing, while others are intended to respawn faster.
  • Bosses: some will have their stats adjusted to the intended difficulty for a five-player party.

For players choosing a server and character, the first restriction is particularly relevant: a shorter deletion period does not mean instant deletion. When questing together, it is also worth checking whether the improved interactions make it easier for every party member to complete an objective.

If you are still choosing where to play, also check the later 8 October announcement of EU servers Nathara and Tassin. We explain their faction assignments and the character transfer announcement there.

What was announced on the morning of 5 October?

The “Thank you Daevas!” message was published on 5 October at 07:04 Poland time (CEST). The team thanks Early Access participants, welcomes new players and says it will take their feedback into account. This is a message accompanying the launch; the maintenance schedule itself was published the previous day.

The morning post does not announce additional rewards or new content; for players planning to log in, the separate technical notice with maintenance times remains the key information.

NCSoft's game is listed on Steam as Free To Play. If you were waiting for the public PC launch, visit the official AION 2 page, check the system requirements and prepare to download the client. When planning to join friends, leave time for installation too; the announced time refers to the servers.

Sources and scope

Our sources are the official maintenance notice dated 4 October and updated on 5 October, the team's morning message and the Steam game page. We cover the planned launch and the announced changes.
