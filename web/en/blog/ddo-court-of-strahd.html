DDO: The Court of Strahd’s 14 October launch, lanterns and Hardcore

Bractwo Krain TeamOriginally published: 4 October 2026English version: 18 October 20263 min read

Dungeons & Dragons Online announced The Court of Strahd, an event in which players build progress in special lanterns. Its launch was scheduled for 14 October alongside Update 82. For players choosing the Hardcore path, one rule is crucial: death ends further progress for the lantern being used.

Developer announcement: 23 September 2026.
Scheduled launch: 14 October 2026 at 19:00 in Poland (CEST, UTC+2).
Status when this article was originally published: the event had not yet started. This is an English translation of our 4 October preview, based on the official DDO announcement checked that day. The translation does not verify the event’s current status.

Where does the event begin?

The announcement says that, once the event opens, players begin by entering the Mists in Korthos, Delera’s Graveyard or the Eveningstar graveyard and completing an introductory quest. The Order of the Guardians will establish nearby outposts. In the story, Strahd is working with a being known as The Caller, and his court is extending the Mists beyond Barovia.

How do lanterns work?

Equipping a lantern will allow you to encounter Strahd’s servants while questing. When the Mists appear, enemies attack. Defeating them fills the lantern, but your character must be close to the Mists when an enemy dies. Full lanterns can be turned in to the Order for rewards. The developers also announced rare, more dangerous ambushes at higher levels.

Standard lanterns and the Hardcore path

Two progression paths were announced: standard and Hardcore. A Hardcore lantern must be equipped at character level 1. Any death permanently prevents that particular lantern from gaining further progress.

Check this condition before choosing a path. If your main goal is to explore the event, do not decide based solely on the Hardcore name: read the rules available at launch. The announcement itself does not confirm that previously accumulated progress is deleted, or explain how rewards can be claimed after death. It does not describe death as the loss of the character itself.

What was confirmed, and what remained to be announced?

Our source is “The Court of Strahd Arrives October 14th!”, published by the DDO team on 23 September. The announced time of 13:00 Eastern (UTC−4) corresponds to 19:00 in Poland (CEST, UTC+2). The developers promised more information at launch and a new leaderboard system. The announcement did not provide an event end date or a complete reward list.

Before playing, check the latest developer announcement. This article covers the announced rules, rather than impressions from the live event.
