AION 2 World Market and World Exchange open November 25

Bractwo Krain TeamOriginally published: 22 November 2026English version updated: 26 November 20263 min read

An archived preview of AION 2 Global’s World Market and World Exchange, originally published on 22 November. It covers the announced trading conditions, tax and listing limits, preserving the information available before the update.

Status checked on 26 November: World Market and World Exchange appear in the 25 November patch notes, and NC has confirmed that maintenance is complete and the servers are back online. The launch date discussed in this archive has passed; consult the publisher’s full notes for current details.

Source date: NC published its announcement on 21 November 2026 at 08:00 CET (07:00 UTC). Our article is published on 22 November.

When do the new markets open?

NC specifies 25 November 2026, after maintenance. The official announcement covers the global service without giving an opening hour. Check maintenance notices before scheduling a session; the date alone does not promise access from midnight.

Confirmed trading conditions

ConditionNC’s announcement
ReachOther servers within your faction and region
AccessMembership users
Sales tax20% in both systems
Sale listings10 daily on each: World Market and World Exchange (Quna)
Existing tradingServer Market and Server Exchange remain available

Check reach using two filters: faction and region. “World” does not remove those boundaries. When comparing advice and offers, remember that observations from another region may not reflect your character’s market.

What remains after the 20% tax?

Our worked example: a sale price of 1,000 units incurs 200 in tax, leaving 800. To receive 1,000 after that tax, the price would need to be 1,250. This calculation includes only the announced 20%; the notice does not describe other costs.

For crafting, compare proceeds after tax with the value of materials consumed. A listing price is not profit, and an unsold item generates no proceeds. Set your minimum acceptable return before listing.

How to prepare your listings

These are our practical suggestions, rather than a price forecast:

  1. Record current prices. Once trading opens, compare local listings with the new market and calculate proceeds. Wider reach alone does not guarantee a better sale.
  2. Choose priorities. With limited listings, prepare a shortlist instead of assuming you can sell your entire storage immediately.
  3. Evaluate access. Before buying Membership solely for trading, check the product conditions in your region and how often you expect to sell.

Still unspecified: the notice does not explain resets, whether limits attach to accounts or characters, or the full eligible-item list. Do not assume additional characters multiply your available listings.

Latest notice: 7,947 accounts restricted

A separate NC notice published on 21 November at 23:00 CET reports 7,947 restricted accounts. It does not describe every sanction as permanent and distinguishes sanctions from security locks. Our AION 2 RMT guide explains the rules and appeals.
