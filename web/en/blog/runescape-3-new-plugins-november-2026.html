RuneScape 3 — 13 new plugins and which ones to enable

Bractwo Krain Team Published 4 November 2026 English version first published 4 November 2026 Updated 9 November 2026 4 min read

Jagex released 13 new RuneScape 3 plugins on 5 November 2026. Ground Items helps you spot loot during combat, Drop Log shows how a trip paid off, and Farming Timer tracks when to return to your patches. The second batch also includes technical dependencies that some of the plugins use.[5]

All 13 new RS3 plugins

PluginWhat does it do?
Ground ItemsLabels items on the ground and lets you highlight them by name, value or ownership.
Drop LogLogs drops, their value and the supplies you used. Useful for comparing the results of different trips.
Farming TimerTracks when crops and Player-Owned Farm animals are ready. Requires the in-game clock to be visible.
Unified LogsBrings collection logs and achievements into one searchable progress view.
Perk CalcEstimates the odds and cost of Invention perks for the components you select.
Agility HelperHighlights obstacles and shortcuts and tracks laps, time and experience gained.
HighlighterHighlights selected NPCs, objects and players with adjustable colors and opacity.
True TileMarks the tile an entity actually occupies when its movement animation shows a different position.
WatchdogLets you connect game events to your own on-screen or audio alerts.
UpkeepsAlerts you when a tracked buff expires during PvM combat.
Combat RecorderRecords abilities and other combat actions in a log you can export.
WikiSyncSends selected character data to the RuneScape Wiki to personalize supported guides and calculators.

These descriptions cover the versions released in the second batch of the official Plugin Hub.[5]

What changed since the preview?

Jagex initially named four approved plugins. The release also included Ground Items, Drop Log and Unified Logs, which were still in review in the preview. Look for the tool originally listed as Entity Highlighter under its release name, Highlighter.[1][5]

Which plugins should you try first?

  • Combat and loot. Try Ground Items if you want to see valuable drops as they land. Use Drop Log if you want to review the entire trip and the cost of the supplies you used.
  • Farming. Enable Farming Timer and make the clock visible on your minimap under Game Clock. The plugin needs the visible in-game time to work.
  • Progression. Pick Agility Helper if you train on obstacle courses, or Unified Logs if you're working through collections and achievements.

If you prefer fewer notifications, start with one plugin and tune its filters. Jagex's official guidelines distinguish presenting information from automating player actions.[4][5]

How do you find and enable plugins in RuneScape 3?

  1. Open the RS Plugin Hub from the toolbox on the Ribbon. In the default modern layout, hover over Settings. You can add the icon to a custom Ribbon in its editor.
  2. Verify your email with mod.io if the Hub prompts you, then search for the plugin by name and install it.
  3. If the plugin is still off after installation, open Manage. Select the padlock, review any permissions it needs, and enable the plugin.

At launch, Jagex reported that some of the new plugins were missing from Browse. If you can't see the one you want, enter its full name in the Hub's search field.[3][5]

For more help with setup, see our RS3 Plugin Hub and Quest Helper guide.

When are plugins coming to Android and iOS?

Jagex plans to support plugins on mobile. Its official 26 October FAQ gave no release date. If you're having an issue with a specific desktop plugin, check its mod.io page for the developer's preferred way to report bugs.[2]
