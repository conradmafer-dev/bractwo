RuneScape 3: four new plugins approved, with release targeted for 5 November

Bractwo Krain TeamPublished: 4 November 2026English version first published: 4 November 20263 min read

The next RuneScape 3 plugin batch is targeted for 5 November 2026. Jagex plans new releases every two weeks, giving players a regular opportunity to revisit the Plugin Hub and check for additions.[2]

Jagex has approved these four plugins. Approval does not yet confirm in-game availability. A detailed feature breakdown is planned for release day.

What is still waiting?

Ground Items, Watchdog, Upkeeps, WikiSync and Museum Quiz need changes after their initial review. Unified Logs, Drop Log and Farming and PoF Timer still await moderation; they may move to the batch tentatively planned for 19 November.[1]

How to find and enable the Plugin Hub

The official system launched inside the RuneScape client on 19 October. To install an available plugin:

  1. Find the toolbox icon on the Ribbon.
  2. With the default modern interface, hover over Settings. On a customised Ribbon, add the icon through Ribbon editing if it is missing.
  3. Complete the email verification required by mod.io.
  4. Choose and install a plugin. If permissions are required, review them and enable the plugin in the Manage tab.[3]

What can an approved plugin do?

Jagex's guidelines allow tools that organise interfaces, record statistics, support progression planning and display information already available to the player. Plugins cannot perform actions automatically on a player's behalf or reveal hidden information.

Choose additions around a specific need: clearer information on screen, easier organisation or a better record of your progress. A long feature list alone does not tell you how useful a plugin will be during your own sessions.[4]

What about Android and iOS?

Jagex intends to bring plugins to mobile, but has not announced a date. For problems with an individual plugin, use the feedback channel listed by its creator on mod.io. Passing moderation does not guarantee a plugin is free of bugs.[2]
