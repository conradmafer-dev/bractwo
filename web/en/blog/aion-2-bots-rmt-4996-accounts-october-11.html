AION 2 RMT bans: buying Kinah, penalties and account appeals

Bractwo Krain TeamOriginally published: 11 October 20265 min readEnglish version first published: 18 October 2026Updated: 24 October 2026

Buying Kinah or Quna from unauthorized sellers can lead to an AION 2 RMT ban. The global policy lists 30 days for a first RMT offense and 90 days for a second, with escalation possible. Here is what the rules say, how security locks differ and where to appeal.

Can buying Kinah get you banned in AION 2?

Yes. Buyers are covered too. In its 4 October warning about illicit currency, the AION 2 team says buying Kinah or Quna from unauthorized third parties violates its terms and can lead to loss of game access.

RMT means real-money trading: exchanging game currency or items for real-world payment. A currency seller's promise of a safe transaction does not establish permission from the publisher. This guide covers the global service and its AION2 Operation Policy; use the rules for your own region.

How long does an AION 2 RMT ban last?

The global policy's Real-Money Trading (RMT) row lists:

OffenseGame access restriction
First30 days
Second90 days

The policy allows increased sanctions and recovery of related gains. These durations are not a guarantee for every case; NC's illicit-currency warning also allows termination of access. Check the category and period in your own notice.

How to appeal an AION 2 account restriction

The global Operation Policy requires an appeal within 15 days of the sanction. Use official AION 2 Customer Support and its contact option.

  1. Save the exact restriction message, the date it appeared and any reference number.
  2. State your region, server and character, then explain which decision you want reviewed and why you believe it is incorrect.
  3. Attach relevant records or a screenshot through the official support form and retain the ticket number.

These preparation steps are our editorial advice. NC says requests are reviewed individually; an appeal does not guarantee that a sanction will be reversed. If the notice describes a protective security lock, ask support about restoring access under that process.

The 11 October case: 4,996 accounts restricted

On 11 October 2026 at 08:00 CEST, NC announced restrictions on 4,996 accounts. This is the historical figure from that action, not a running total.

The official NC announcement lists bots, unauthorized software and macros, organized gold farming and real-money trading, usually abbreviated to RMT. The action also covers transactions that disrupt the game's economy.

According to the publisher, penalties follow a verification process. Normal gameplay, including long experience-grinding sessions, does not warrant sanctions. The figure of 4,996 refers to accounts subject to restrictions; the announcement does not say that all of them received permanent bans.

Security locks are a separate issue

NC distinguishes penalties from temporary protective locks applied when an account may be at risk. A protective lock is not a cheating penalty. Players who believe a restriction is a mistake, or who want to regain access after a security lock, should contact official AION 2 support.

Archive: the original October character transfer announcement

Following the announcement of the new EU servers Nathara and Tassin covered here, NC published the specific transfer conditions. The 9 October announcement sets out two windows using European dates:

  • Early Access: from the 14 October maintenance until the start of maintenance on 21 October.
  • Launch servers: from the 21 October maintenance until the start of maintenance on 28 October.

A transfer costs 0 Quna and is available once per account, within the same faction and region. Early Access characters can only move to other Early Access servers. Any additional character slots needed cost 1,000 Quna per slot.

Check your server storage: its items will not move with your character. NC recommends moving them to your inventory or character storage beforehand. Before transferring, check the full transfer requirements, including character level, Legion membership and unclaimed mail.
