WoW Forever: Alterac Valley changes on November 25

Bractwo Krain TeamOriginally published: 26 November 2026English version: 26 November 20263 min read

Blizzard has changed rewards and enemies in WoW: Forever’s Alterac Valley. Killing the General gives less Honor and reputation, while capturing objectives carries more weight. Here is what to consider before your next match.

Announcement date: 25 November 2026 at 20:42 CET (19:42 UTC). Blizzard describes these changes as implemented. Our article is published on 26 November and covers WoW: Forever only.

How are rewards changing?

Kaivax’s official announcement describes three shifts:

  • The opposing faction’s General: significantly less Honor and reputation for defeating them.
  • Towers, Graveyards and Mines: more reputation and a small Honor increase.
  • Enemy NPCs: more reputation from kills.

Blizzard intends to keep Honor broadly similar and increase reputation per match. That is the stated goal. The announcement provides neither measurements from completed matches nor hourly rates. It also does not give the exact new values for individual rewards.

Four buff stacks and tougher commanders

Enemy creatures have more health and deal more damage. Generals and Warmasters/Marshals gain an objective-related buff that starts with four stacks and lasts 20 minutes. Each captured objective removes one stack.

Warmasters/Marshals react to players from further away and leash when they leave their rooms. Generals hit harder, detect stealthed characters more effectively, and have received a fix for a bug that left some of them less defensive than intended.

How should you approach your next match?

Our practical interpretation: reassess objective control and group readiness before attacking commanders. A route memorized before the changes does not give you a complete picture of the current risk.

  1. Agree on objectives with your team. Check which points you are taking and who will stay with them. A large group moving toward the enemy base does not tell you how many buff stacks its defenders still have.
  2. Prepare to engage together. Account for the larger aggro range and commanders returning to their rooms when positioning your group. Be particularly careful with plans built around pulling enemies away or approaching in stealth.
  3. Assess the whole session. Record Honor, reputation, match duration and queue time. Comparing several games tells you more than the reward from one General; the result may depend on how the battle develops and how well your team coordinates.

For example, higher reputation per match can coexist with slower progress over an hour if games take longer. That is an arithmetic relationship, not a measurement we have made since the update. We therefore do not turn Blizzard’s stated intention into a promise of faster reputation gains.
