VAMPIR: level 70 Crest and EU Skirmish on November 28–29

Bractwo Krain TeamOriginally published: 22 November 2026English version: 22 November 20264 min read

A level 70 Crest slot and the first Skirmish are among global VAMPIR’s upcoming additions. The newest Netmarble notices used here were published on 20 November. We separate the update preview from the battle schedule, convert EU times to CET and explain what the announced Diamond bonuses mean for players.

Dates to save:

  • 24 November: the update date named in the preview; it is still upcoming when this article is published.
  • 28–29 November: the first Skirmish, from 19:30 to 20:00 CET on EU servers.

Crest, Sigil and the 30-day anniversary

The 20 November preview describes Crest as an accessory slot unlocked at level 70, with items available through sources including crafting and Serpent’s Throne. Sigil’s event will connect monster drops, equipment enhancement and exchanges for chests. Events celebrating 30 days since launch include 14 days of login rewards.

Our advice: once the full rules are available, compare the cost of a particular Crest with your other upgrades. A new slot alone does not tell you which item suits your class. Below the required level, plan your progression before buying materials. For Sigil’s event, check exchange requirements and end dates before deciding how much to spend on enhancing event equipment.

The preview may change. Confirm exact rewards, costs and maintenance hours in the final update notice.

When is Skirmish on EU servers?

The first Skirmish rules qualify the top three clans by Clan Battle Power on each server for the preliminary round. Winners advance to a final between paired servers. The EU schedule gives 21:30–22:00 at UTC+3, which converts to:

EU Skirmish — CET (UTC+1), including Poland
RoundDateTime
PreliminarySaturday, 28 November19:30–20:00 CET
FinalSunday, 29 November19:30–20:00 CET

Before arranging a gathering, check your server region, clan position and members’ availability for both evenings. Put the date and time zone in the invitation, particularly with an international roster. Plan a qualification check and a separate final roster: Saturday attendance does not establish who can play on Sunday. These converted times apply to EU servers.

How many bonus Diamonds does EU receive?

The bonus event announced on 20 November increases the Marketplace tax prize pool. These are bonus amounts for the listed servers, rather than the entire pool or an individual player’s payout.

Amounts apply to each listed server, not to the whole group combined. Rewards go to clan storage. In the final, 60% of the pool goes to the winner and 40% is distributed according to participating clans’ points.

Our practical suggestion for organisers: plan participation separately from expected earnings. Agree how clan resources will be used before committing to shared expenses, and explain the reward rules to members. Dividing a table amount by your roster size does not produce a reliable personal payout forecast. Smaller clans should assess their qualification prospects before planning preparations that require extra resources.

An AMA before the battle weekend

The 20 November AMA notice lists 25 November at 19:30 UTC−5: 26 November at 01:30 CET. The Discord session is intended to cover Skirmish, future updates and community questions.
